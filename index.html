p.2 — what happened

Woke up where the walls sweat.

No dramatic intro. Tile, damp, a buzzing terminal. The file on screen described the room I was standing in — word for word. Dark. Locked. Silent.

I changed one word and the ██████ changed with it. I'm not writing which word first. You'll do it wrong.

The halls after that don't behave. Some rooms are kind: a fire, rain on glass. Some lean wrong and you feel it behind your teeth before you see anything. I started marking the kind ones on the back of my hand.

  • § 01 — there is more than one way out of every room. The green lever taught me that.
  • § 02 — everything has something to say if you look twice. Even the soap.
  • § 03 — if your gut pulls, follow it. Sanity goes fast down here.

P.S. if you find my spare battery, keep it. you'll need it.

p.3 — method, as I understand it

Three steps. Then improvise.

i.

Read the room like a draft

Check the terminal text against what's actually there. Door says wooden [locked]? The brackets are the lie. Note the exact wording.

door: wooden [locked]

ii.

Cross it out in ink

One word at a time. Locked → open. Dark → lit. Don't get clever on your first night. Small edits hold better.

door = “open”

iii.

Live with what you wrote

Heavy box through the door works. So does the side window nobody mentions. The Komplex remembers which you picked.

result: yours

Leveling, no fighting. Three quiet skills go up as you poke around. Good for people who hate combat but like dread.

Timeline on the wall. I kept a tally per room so I'd know what I skipped. Bathroom took me two days. Soap. That's all I'll say.

Comfort is a resource. Fire, rain, a working lamp. Sit a minute. Your head clears. Then keep moving.

— east wing. don't linger.

p.6 — words we use down here

Margin lexicon.

Copied from the back of my hand. Spelling is the least of our problems.

ink — n.

Whatever's wet on the terminal. Cross a word out and the room obeys. Mostly.

sanity — n.

The flame. Fire and rain feed it. Darkness spends it. Spend wisely.

battery — n.

Small, hoarded, never enough. The terminal eats them. Keep one spare. Always.

soap — n.

Bathroom. Not soap. That is the whole entry. You'll see.

lever — n.

Green means go. There are two. Pulling both means 100%. Pull both.

the pull — n.

The tug behind your teeth when a room leans wrong. Follow it. It's never wrong about danger, only about timing.

p.9 — asked often, answered once

Before you ask.

How scary are we talking?

No jumpscare reel. It's the slow kind — a hallway that feels wrong before anything happens. Headphones help. So does pausing to breathe.

How long is the demo?

One careful evening. Two if you 100% every room like I tried. The bathroom alone took me two days.

Windows only, really?

Really. The demo itself is a Windows download — the little terminal on this page is just a toy I taped here. Small file, runs on tired laptops.

I got eaten. Is that normal?

Completely. Dying is how the Komplex teaches. The right-hand dark took me three times before I learned. Take the left.

What do I type first?

Read the terminal first — always. Then small words. Locked → open is the classic for a reason.
